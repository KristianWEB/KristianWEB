Verbatim from the LWB webinar ads:

- "FYI it won't let you register because it is not accepting the phone number added. Tried in several formats and nothing worked to get past this step." (funnel bug, still open from the Sep 21 handoff, fix it)

The pattern: the current ads say "paper losses, income redirected, money positioned" and stop. Skeptics read that as vague, and the ones who comment are exactly the buyer (W-2, high income, already maxing the 401k). They are asking for the mechanism. The next lane answers them.

Two things change in this lane. The ad opens with a real comment on screen, and the ad names one mechanism plainly, with its conditions. The old rule was "specific about the problem, vague about the solution." For this lane the solution gets specific, because that is the ask.

Prior ruling to be aware of: single-tactic and STR-centered ads were killed in July on lead quality ("the viewer remembers STR tax hack, not LWB"). This lane tests that ruling on purpose, as a comment response, one cell at a time, judged on Hyros cost per call and sales.
